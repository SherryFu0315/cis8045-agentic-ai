Logistics

Meetings, tools, grading and policies.

Meetings

CIS 8045 meets one evening a week during Mini-Mester 1, 5:30 to 9:45 p.m., at the Buckhead Center. The day and the room will appear here once the section is posted. The last session falls in the exam period and is used for the final presentations.

Prerequisites

At least one earlier PATH course. Python helps on the code path and is not needed on the no-code path. Bring a laptop you can install software on.

Tools and accounts

Everything in the course runs on free tiers. Before the first class, create accounts for LangSmith (tracing, evaluation and Fleet), GitHub, and one model provider with a free tier. A setup guide with the exact steps will be posted on iCollege. Local models through Ollama are an option for anyone who prefers not to send data to a provider.

Using AI in this course

You may use any AI tool for any part of the work except the individual reflection. Two conditions apply. Disclose what you used and for what in each deliverable, in a sentence or two. And be ready to explain any line of code or any paragraph you submit. The oral trace check exists for that reason. If you cannot explain a piece of work, it will not count.

Late work and absences

Weekly deliverables are team work and are due before the next session. A team may submit one of them up to a week late without penalty by telling me before the deadline. With eight sessions, each class carries a lot of weight, so tell me in advance if you must miss one and we will arrange how you make up the lab.

Academic honesty

The Georgia State Student Code of Conduct applies to this course. The AI rules above say what is allowed. Copying another team's work, exposing a sponsor's confidential data, or submitting undisclosed AI output are violations.

Accommodations

Students who wish to request accommodation for a disability may do so by registering with the Access and Accommodation Center. Students may only be accommodated upon issuance by the Access and Accommodation Center of a signed Accommodation Plan and are responsible for providing a copy of that plan to instructors of all classes in which accommodations are sought. The center is in Student Center East, Suite 304 (access.gsu.edu, 404-413-1560).
